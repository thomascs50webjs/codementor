Usage Policy & Guidelines

Usage Policies

To ensure safe and responsible use of this platform, all users are required to adhere to the following guidelines. Violations may result in content removal, temporary suspension, or permanent account ban.

Prohibited Uses
  1. Creating, sharing, or executing illegal or malicious code, including malware, viruses, exploits, or hacking tools.
  2. Violating any law, regulation, or the rights of others, including intellectual property and data privacy laws.
  3. Distributing or hosting harmful content, such as scripts for phishing, credential theft, or unauthorized access.
  4. Attempting to bypass platform restrictions, moderation systems, or security measures.
  5. Harming, disrupting, or abusing other users or systems.
Enforcement Actions
  1. Content removal or takedown.
  2. Account warnings or limitations.
  3. Temporary suspension for repeated or serious violations.
  4. Permanent ban for egregious misuse.
Reporting, Appeals & Updates
  1. If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error, you may contact support to request a review.
  2. These policies may be updated at any time. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the latest version.
